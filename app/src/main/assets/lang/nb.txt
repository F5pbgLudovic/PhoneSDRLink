Sensitivity (1 to 10): the higher the number, the weaker the signals the decoder accepts (start code, sync pulses, lines), at the risk of a few false detections. Data loss (1 to 10): how long the decoder keeps going with a picture when the signal disappears (3 s at 1, 12 s at 4, 120 s at 10); if the signal comes back in that time, the picture resumes at the right line and the lost lines stay grey; after that time the picture is saved as it is and the decoder listens again (it then finds the mode by itself).
